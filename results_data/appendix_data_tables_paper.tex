\subsection{Adjusting for GPU Price-Performance Gains}
Trends in benchmark price-performance are also influenced by hardware performance trends. If we want to isolate the component due purely to algorithmic advances, we have to divide the annual factor decrease by the annual hardware price-efficiency gain. Here we use our general regression approach and estimates from \citet{epoch2024priceperformancehardware}, which finds that for a fixed performance level, costs have dropped by $30\%$ a year.
